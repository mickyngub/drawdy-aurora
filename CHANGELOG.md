# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- four patterns: Curtains, Glyphs (a textmode wave of rings and crosses), Dots and Contours
- two more starting points: Mono and Neon
- the scale slider is named for the pattern: curtain height, cell size, dot spacing or line spacing

## 0.2.0

- the aurora button now opens a panel; the first time, it also turns the aurora on
- six starting points: Aurora, Ocean, Sunset, Nebula, Ember and Quiet
- sliders for strength, speed, colour shift, curtain height and haze, applied to the board as you drag
- Reset goes back to the chosen starting point
- your look and on or off are kept, so the aurora comes back the next time you open Drawdy

## 0.1.0

- the aurora button turns a slow WebGL aurora on and off across your whole board
- clicks, drags, scrolling and typing pass straight through to the board
- a soft wash that your drawings read through, kept lighter behind the top toolbar
- follows Drawdy's light or dark theme, with a still glow when your system asks for reduced motion
