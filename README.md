# Aurora ˚₊‧꒰ა ☆ ໒꒱ ‧₊˚

**let your board glow.**

Slow northern lights drift across your whole board while you draw. It's a soft wash over everything, light enough that your drawings read through it, and the board works exactly as before.

---

## let's glow ٩(◕‿◕)۶

1. Click the **aurora waves** in the extension rail.
2. The aurora fades in across the whole screen.
3. Draw, select, type and pan as usual: every click goes straight through to the board.
4. Click the waves again to turn it off.

> (・_・;) **nothing shows?** If your system asks for reduced motion, Aurora shows a still glow instead of a moving one.

---

**the small print**: nothing downloads and nothing goes on your board; only you see the aurora. It follows Drawdy's light or dark theme. Code [MIT](https://github.com/mickyngub/drawdy-aurora/blob/main/LICENSE); building it yourself? See [DEVELOPMENT.md](https://github.com/mickyngub/drawdy-aurora/blob/main/DEVELOPMENT.md).
