# Aurora ˚₊‧꒰ა ☆ ໒꒱ ‧₊˚

**let your board glow.**

Slow northern lights rise from the bottom of your screen while you draw. The top bar, the corners and the middle stay clear, and the board works exactly as before.

---

## let's glow ٩(◕‿◕)۶

1. Click the **aurora waves** in the extension rail.
2. The glow fades in along the bottom of your screen.
3. Draw, select, type and pan as usual: every click goes straight through to the board.
4. Click the waves again to turn it off.

> (・_・;) **nothing shows?** If your system asks for reduced motion, Aurora shows a still glow instead of a moving one.

---

**the small print**: nothing downloads and nothing goes on your board; only you see the aurora. It follows Drawdy's light or dark theme.
